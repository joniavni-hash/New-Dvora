# Villa Lithos Publishing Rules

## Platform-Specific Image Guidelines

### 🎵 TikTok
- ✅ **Use overlay images** (final-1.png, final-2.png, etc.)
- ✅ **Text overlays allowed** - helps with engagement
- ✅ **Multiple slides** work well for carousel effect
- ✅ **Graphic elements** enhance the content

### 📷 Instagram 
- ❌ **NO text overlays** on images
- ✅ **Use clean background images** (slide-1-bg.jpg, slide-2-bg.jpg, etc.)
- ✅ **Pure visuals** - let the caption do the talking
- ✅ **High-quality photography** showcasing the villa

### 🔵 Facebook
- ❌ **NO text overlays** on images  
- ✅ **Use clean background images** (slide-1-bg.jpg, slide-2-bg.jpg, etc.)
- ✅ **Natural photos** work best
- ✅ **Let the post text carry the message**

### 📌 Pinterest
- ✅ **Text overlays encouraged** - Pinterest loves informative pins
- ✅ **Use final-X.png** images with overlays
- ✅ **Descriptive graphics** perform well
