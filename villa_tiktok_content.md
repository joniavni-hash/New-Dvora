# Villa Lithos TikTok Content Plan

## Hook: Luxury Villa Value Proposition
**Target:** Group travelers (12-22 people)

## 6-Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Hook
**Text:** "My friends think I'm rich, but this luxury villa in Greece costs less per person than most hotels"
**Visual:** Villa exterior with pool

### Slide 2: Scale & Luxury
**Text:** "22 people, 9 bedrooms, private infinity pool, 15 min from Athens airport"  
**Visual:** Infinity pool with sea view

### Slide 3: Unique Features
**Text:** "Private paddle court, sauna, 5000m² of pure luxury"
**Visual:** Paddle court or luxury interior

### Slide 4: Value Calculation  
**Text:** "€8000 per week ÷ 22 people = €360 per person for THE WHOLE WEEK"
**Visual:** Calculator or price comparison graphic

### Slide 5: Hotel Comparison
**Text:** "Meanwhile hotels in Mykonos charge €300+ per NIGHT per room"
**Visual:** Expensive hotel room vs villa luxury

### Slide 6: Call to Action
**Text:** "Villa Lithos Porto Rafti - link in bio"
**Visual:** Villa logo with contact info

## Implementation Plan
1. Download images from villa website  
2. Create text overlays using Canva (free)
3. Export as 9:16 vertical videos
4. Post manually to TikTok with trending audio
5. Track performance via built-in TikTok analytics

## Success Metrics
- Views: Target 10K+ for first post
- Engagement: Comments asking about booking
- Website clicks from bio link
