# App Category Templates

Specialized prompts and hooks for different app categories.

## Home Design Apps

### Visual Prompts
```
iPhone photo of a [room type], [current state - tired/outdated/basic], 
natural lighting, lived-in feel with everyday objects (books, mugs, phone charger),
realistic apartment setting, taken on iPhone 15 Pro. Portrait orientation.

Transformation: same room layout, same window placement, same architectural features,
but [style upgrade - modern/cozy/luxurious], professional interior design quality,
magazine-worthy styling, aspirational but achievable.
```

### Hook Templates
- "My landlord said I can't change anything... challenge accepted"
- "POV: You have [budget] to make this room not look like a college dorm"
- "My [person] said our [room] looks like [insult] so I used AI to prove them wrong"
- "Showing my landlord what our apartment could look like"
- "This [room] has been bothering me for [time period]"

### Pain Points to Address
- Rental restrictions (can't paint, can't renovate)
- Budget constraints ($200-500 transformation)
- Partner/roommate skepticism  
- Landlord approval needed
- Small space limitations

## Text Overlay Adaptations by Category

### Home Design
- Focus on **transformation shock** ("Wait, is this the same room??")
- Use **rental/budget constraints** as tension
- Include **authority figures** (landlord, partner) for validation

### Beauty  
- Focus on **confidence boost** ("I actually feel pretty")
- Use **event pressure** as urgency (date, wedding, photos)
- Include **social validation** (compliments, reactions)

### Fitness
- Focus on **capability growth** ("I can finally...")  
- Use **past failures** as contrast ("I've tried everything")
- Include **measurable progress** (timeframes, visible changes)

### Productivity
- Focus on **chaos to control** ("My life was a mess")
- Use **professional consequences** (boss reactions, deadlines)
- Include **life balance** improvements (stress relief, free time)

### Food
- Focus on **skill development** ("I couldn't even boil water")
- Use **expense/health** as motivation (money saved, energy gained)  
- Include **social occasions** (cooking for others, impressive results)

## Cross-Platform Adaptations

### TikTok
- Fast-paced, high energy
- Trending sounds essential
- Personal stories work best

### Instagram Reels  
- Aesthetic focus important
- Hashtags matter more
- Longer captions acceptable

### YouTube Shorts
- Educational angle helpful  
- How-to elements work well
- Subscribe CTAs effective
