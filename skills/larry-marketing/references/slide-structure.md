# 6-Slide TikTok Structure

## The Formula That Works

Based on Larry's 1M+ view slideshows, this structure stops the scroll:

### Slide 1: Hook + Setup
**Purpose:** Stop the scroll in first 0.5 seconds
**Text examples:**
- "I showed my landlord what AI thinks our kitchen should look like"
- "My boyfriend said our flat looks basic so I used AI to prove him wrong"
- "POV: You have $500 and 2 hours to transform your room"

**Visual:** Before state or setup shot

### Slide 2: Challenge/Problem
**Purpose:** Create tension, relate to audience pain point
**Text examples:** 
- "She said I can't change anything... challenge accepted"
- "The budget was tight but I had an idea"
- "This room has been bothering me for months"

**Visual:** Problem highlighted or constraint shown

### Slide 3: Solution Introduction
**Purpose:** Present your app/solution as the hero
**Text examples:**
- "So I downloaded this app and took one photo"
- "I found this AI tool that changes everything"
- "This app literally reads your mind"

**Visual:** App in action or transformation beginning

### Slide 4: Transformation/Result
**Purpose:** Show the "wow" moment, build aspiration
**Text examples:**
- "Wait... is this actually the same kitchen??"
- "I can't believe this is the same room"
- "The AI literally understood my vision"

**Visual:** After state or dramatic improvement

### Slide 5: Reaction/Validation  
**Purpose:** Social proof, relatability, emotional peak
**Text examples:**
- "Okay I'm literally obsessed with this one"
- "My landlord actually asked for the app name"
- "Even my boyfriend was impressed"

**Visual:** Best result or multiple options

### Slide 6: CTA + App Name
**Purpose:** Drive action while excitement is high
**Text examples:**
- "Snugly showed me what's possible - link in bio"
- "Download RoomAI - it's free to try"
- "Search 'HomePlus' on the App Store"

**Visual:** Final result with subtle app branding

## Text Overlay Rules

### Formatting
- **4-6 words per line maximum**
- **Use \\n for manual line breaks**
- **3-4 lines per slide ideal**
- **No emoji** (canvas rendering issues)

### Positioning
- **28% from top** (safe zone)
- **White text, thick black outline**
- **Dynamic sizing:** short text = bigger font

### Voice & Tone
- **Reactions, not labels** ("Wait, this is nice??" not "Modern style")
- **Conversational** ("I literally can't even" not "Subsequently")
- **Questions and ellipses** create engagement
- **Personal pronouns** (I, my, you)

## Hook Categories That Work

### 1. Person + Conflict + AI
"My [relationship] said [criticism] so I used AI to [prove them wrong]"

Examples:
- "My boyfriend said our flat looks basic so I used AI..."
- "My mom said I have no design sense so I let AI..."

### 2. Challenge/Constraint
"I have [limitation] to transform [space]"

Examples:  
- "POV: You have $200 to make this room not depressing"
- "Landlord said no painting... watch this"

### 3. Before/After Shock
"Showing my [person] what [space] could look like"

Examples:
- "Showing my landlord what our kitchen could look like"
- "I showed my roommates what AI thinks of our living room"

### 4. Relatable Pain Point
"This [problem] has been bothering me for [time]"

Examples:
- "This corner has been empty for 6 months"
- "I've stared at this blank wall for too long"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "This is my living room renovation" | "My boyfriend said our living room looks like a waiting room" |
| Generic descriptions | Personal reactions with emotion |
| Long text blocks | 4-6 words per line, manual breaks |
| Weak CTA | Specific action + urgency |
| Same room setup | Lock architecture but vary styling |
| Obvious AI images | iPhone photo prompts + realistic lighting |

## App Category Adaptations

### Home Design Apps
Focus on: Rental restrictions, landlord approval, budget constraints, partner skepticism

### Beauty/Filter Apps  
Focus on: Event preparation, confidence boost, friend reactions, trying new looks

### Fitness Apps
Focus on: Transformation journey, consistency struggles, motivation, visible progress

### Productivity Apps
Focus on: Chaos to organization, time management, life hacks, efficiency gains

## Performance Benchmarks

- **50K+ views:** Viral hook - make 3 variations immediately
- **10K-50K views:** Good hook - keep in rotation  
- **1K-10K views:** Test 1-2 more variations
- **<1K views twice:** Drop hook category, try something radically different

## Music Integration

**Critical:** Add trending audio after upload
- Browse TikTok's sound library in your niche
- Pick sounds with 10K+ uses that are <7 days old
- Avoid copyrighted music (gets flagged)
- Silent slideshows get buried by algorithm
