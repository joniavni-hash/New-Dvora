## Audio Strategy

### TikTok Audio Selection Criteria
- **Trending sounds** — check TikTok Creative Center for current trending audio
- **Travel/luxury niche audio** — sounds already popular in villa/travel content
- **Voiceover option** — TikTok text-to-speech or custom voiceover

### Recommended Audio Approaches (pick one per post)
| Option | Audio | When to Use |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| A — Trending Sound | Current viral audio from Creative Center | Default — maximizes reach |
| B — Narration | TikTok TTS reading the slide text | When clarity matters more than virality |
| C — Ambient + Music | Soft Mediterranean/chill beat | For premium feel, lower viral potential |

### How to Find Trending Audio
1. Open TikTok Creative Center → https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/
2. Go to "Trending" → "Songs"
3. Filter: Region = UK, Period = 7 days
4. Pick audio with 50K-500K uses (sweet spot: trending but not oversaturated)
5. Or: scroll "For You" page, save audio from high-performing travel/lifestyle posts

## Posting Times — UK Audience Optimization

### Primary Windows (UK timezone = GMT/BST)
| Slot | UK Time | Israel Time | Why |
|------|---------|-------------|-----|
| **Morning commute** | 7:30-8:30 AM | 9:30-10:30 AM | Scrolling on transport |
| **Lunch break** | 12:00-1:00 PM | 2:00-3:00 PM | Mid-day scroll |
| **Evening prime** | 7:00-9:00 PM | 9:00-11:00 PM | Peak TikTok usage |

### Best Single Time to Post
**UK 7:30 PM (Israel 9:30 PM)** — consistently highest TikTok engagement for UK audience

### Posting Frequency (Pilot Phase)
- **3-4 posts over 48 hours** — test different times
- Never more than 2 posts per day on same account
- Minimum 4 hours between posts
